About KB Dental Care Referral Service

Dedicated to simplifying access to qualified dental specialists, reducing appointment delays, and connecting patients across Kathmandu Valley with verified clinics.

Our Role and Healthcare Mission

When acute tooth pain strikes or when you need a complex restorative treatment such as dental implants or orthodontic braces, navigating the hundreds of private dental practices across Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur can be overwhelming. Patients often struggle to evaluate sterilization standards, verify physician credentials, or find clinics with open chair availability without calling clinic after clinic.

KB Dental was founded to bridge this gap. We operate as an independent dental patient referral and care coordination network based in Madhyapur Thimi, Kathmandu Valley. We evaluate independent dental clinics, verify that practicing clinicians hold active registration with the Nepal Medical Council (NMC), and match patients directly with the most appropriate local clinic based on proximity, clinical specialty, and urgent appointment availability.

Important Healthcare Relationship Disclosure

KB Dental is a referral and patient coordination service, not a direct medical clinic or dental hospital. We do not personally provide dental examinations, clinical diagnoses, surgeries, or prescriptions. All clinical services are delivered independently by third-party dental clinics and licensed dentists registered with the Nepal Medical Council. Independent clinics determine their own medical diagnoses, clinical protocols, and treatment fees. Our referral matching service is 100% free to patients.

Our Vetting and Quality Standards

We only refer patients to independent dental facilities in Kathmandu Valley that meet four clear criteria:

1. Licensed Dental Surgeons

All partner dentists hold recognized Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) or Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) degrees with verified active registration with the Nepal Medical Council.

2. Autoclave Sterilization

Partner operatories follow international infection control protocols, utilizing Class-B vacuum autoclaves, biological monitoring, individually pouched instruments, and medical-grade surface disinfectants.

3. Modern Diagnostic Tools

Clinics are equipped with digital Radiovisiography (RVG) intraoral X-ray sensors, digital apex locators for root canal treatments, and access to 3D CBCT bone imaging for surgical implants.

4. Transparent Fee Estimates

Partner clinics provide upfront, itemized estimates before commencing restorative work such as dental crowns or tooth fillings, with no hidden surcharges.

Geographic Service Area Across Kathmandu Valley

Our coordination desk assists patients throughout the three districts of Kathmandu Valley:

  • Central Kathmandu: New Road, Putalisadak, Thamel, Bagbazar, and Sundhara.
  • Lalitpur & Patan: Jawalakhel, Pulchowk, Kumaripati, and Sanepa.
  • Bhaktapur & Madhyapur Thimi: Our home hub serving Thimi, Lokanthali, Kaushaltar, and Sallaghari.
  • New Baneshwor: Mid-valley transit hub covering Minbhawan, Shantinagar, and Tinkune.
  • Maharajgunj & North: Baluwatar, Lazimpat, Chakrapath, and Samakhusi.
