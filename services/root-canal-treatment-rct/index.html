Save your natural tooth, permanently remove bacterial nerve infections, and achieve swift relief from severe, throbbing dental pain.

What is Root Canal Treatment (RCT)? Direct Answer

Root Canal Treatment (RCT), or endodontic therapy, is a dental procedure designed to remove inflamed or necrotic pulp tissue from inside a tooth's root canals. When deep cavities, repeated dental procedures, or tooth trauma expose the delicate nerves and blood vessels to bacteria, an intense infection develops. Rather than extracting the tooth, an experienced dentist in Kathmandu cleans, shapes, and disinfects the root chambers before sealing them hermetically with biocompatible gutta-percha. In modern practices across Kathmandu Valley, rotary endodontic motors and digital apex locators make the entire treatment painless, predictable, and frequently completed in just 1 to 2 clinical visits.

Signs and Symptoms You May Need a Root Canal

Patients seeking an emergency dental clinic in Kathmandu often report common warning signals. Because nerve degeneration can progress from initial hypersensitivity to severe periapical abscess, you should watch for these classic indications:

  • Lingering Sensitivity: Pain that persists for 10–30 seconds after consuming hot tea, coffee, cold water, or ice cream.
  • Severe Spontaneous Throbbing: Deep, radiating ache in the jaw or ear that strikes without provocation, particularly when lying flat in bed at night.
  • Biting Tenderness: Sharp discomfort or bruised sensations when chewing hard foods or tapping against the tooth.
  • Gum Swelling & Sinus Tracts: A pimple-like bump (fistula or abscess) on the gum releasing foul-tasting discharge, accompanied by localized puffiness.
  • Tooth Darkening: Greyish discoloration of an anterior or premolar tooth, indicating internal blood vessel necrosis following impact trauma.

Why Saving Your Natural Tooth Matters

When faced with severe toothache, some patients ask whether a tooth extraction is simpler or cheaper. While removing the tooth might provide short-term relief, tooth loss triggers adjacent teeth to shift, destabilizes your bite, causes localized jawbone resorption, and ultimately requires costly replacement with dental implants or bridges. By choosing endodontic therapy with a trusted local Kathmandu dentist, your biological tooth root remains firmly anchored in the periodontal ligament, preserving your natural chewing force and facial structure for decades.

The 4-Step Clinical Root Canal Process

Through KB Dental's referral network, partner clinics utilize modern endodontic protocols designed for maximum patient comfort:

  1. Digital Diagnosis & Painless Anesthesia: High-resolution digital periapical radiographs confirm the root curvature and extent of apical infection. Profound local anesthesia is administered to numb the tooth completely.
  2. Canal Access & Biomechanical Cleaning: A rubber dam isolates the tooth from oral saliva. Microscopic rotary nickel-titanium (NiTi) files thoroughly clear necrotic pulp and bacteria from microscopic root branches.
  3. Antimicrobial Disinfection: Canals are irrigated with warm sodium hypochlorite and ultrasonic activation to destroy persistent microbial colonies within the canal walls.
  4. Hermetic Gutta-Percha Sealing: Once bone dry and asymptomatic, canals are filled with biocompatible gutta-percha cones and sealer cement to block future reinfection. Following RCT, placing a protective dental crown and cap is vital to prevent structural fractures.
