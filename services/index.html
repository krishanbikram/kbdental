How to Choose the Right Treatment

If you are unsure which procedure is needed, consulting an experienced local Kathmandu dentist is the safest first step. During a thorough examination, clinical specialists utilize digital intraoral sensors to inspect hidden interproximal decay, bone density, and pulp vitality before suggesting a customized treatment plan.

For example, minor sensitivity can often be resolved with a conservative composite tooth filling, whereas deep pain that interrupts your sleep typically signifies that you need an emergency root canal in Kathmandu followed by a protective dental crown.
