Dental Implants in Kathmandu Nepal

The premier permanent solution for missing teeth. Restore full chewing power, prevent facial bone collapse, and smile with lifelong confidence.

What is a Dental Implant? Direct Answer

A dental implant is a surgical-grade medical titanium post that is placed directly into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Over 3 to 4 months, a natural biological process known as osseointegration locks the titanium post into living bone tissue. Once fully integrated, a precision abutment and custom-milled zirconia dental crown are attached on top. Unlike removable dentures or conventional dental bridges, dental implants do not rely on adjacent natural teeth for support and stimulate jawbone density just like your real tooth root. When performed by a qualified dentist in Kathmandu with 3D CBCT digital planning, modern dental implants achieve an exceptional long-term success rate exceeding 95%.

Why Dental Implants Outperform Traditional Bridges & Dentures

When a tooth is lost to deep decay, severe periodontal disease, or sports trauma, leaving an empty gap causes adverse physiological consequences. Alveolar bone resorbs rapidly without root stimulation, causing cheeks to hollow and neighboring teeth to tilt into the space.

Dental Implants vs Traditional Bridges

A fixed dental bridge requires grinding away healthy enamel on both neighboring teeth to serve as bridge anchors. In contrast, a dental implant stands completely independent, leaving adjacent natural teeth 100% untouched and intact.

Dental Implants vs Removable Dentures

Removable partial and full dentures frequently slip, cause sore spots on the gums, and deliver only 20–30% of natural chewing efficiency. Dental implants provide 95%+ chewing efficiency, allowing you to eat tough meats, apples, and nuts with zero movement.

The 3 Key Components of a Dental Implant

  1. Titanium Implant Fixture: The screw-threaded artificial root inserted into the jawbone. Formed of commercially pure Grade 4 or Grade 5 titanium, it is entirely biocompatible and hypo-allergenic.
  2. Precision Abutment: The connecting collar that screws securely into the top of the titanium fixture, extending slightly above the gumline to support the prosthetic tooth.
  3. Custom Prosthetic Crown: The visible, tooth-colored replacement sculpted from high-strength monolithic zirconia or ceramic to match the exact shade, contours, and translucency of your natural teeth.

The Step-by-Step Dental Implant Procedure

Through KB Dental's coordination network, you are matched with certified oral surgeons and implantologists in Kathmandu who follow standardized clinical stages:

  1. 3D CBCT Imaging & Surgical Planning: High-resolution 3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scans assess exact bone height, width, and proximity to the mandibular nerve or maxillary sinus.
  2. Implant Placement Surgery: Under localized anesthesia, a small incision is made in the gingiva. A precision osteotomy creates the receptor site, and the titanium fixture is gently threaded into place. A healing screw or suture seals the area.
  3. Osseointegration Period (12–16 Weeks): Bone osteoblasts naturally integrate with the micro-textured titanium surface, creating a permanent structural bond. In low-bone situations, synthetic bone grafting or a sinus lift may be carried out concurrently.
  4. Digital Impression & Crown Placement: An intraoral 3D optical scanner captures the precise position of the abutment without messy impression trays. A dental lab mills a custom zirconia crown that is screwed or cemented into place for immediate function.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure is. Because jawbone tissue has very few nerve pain receptors, placing an implant with local anesthesia typically causes less post-operative soreness than a complicated tooth extraction. Standard over-the-counter pain relievers easily manage any mild tenderness for 48 hours after surgery.

With diligent oral hygiene, daily flossing, and twice-yearly professional dental cleanings to prevent peri-implantitis (gum inflammation around the fixture), dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. The custom zirconia crown on top may occasionally experience normal wear after 15 to 20 years and can easily be replaced without touching the underlying titanium post.

Missing teeth naturally result in bone thinning over time. Fortunately, modern implantology in Kathmandu routinely performs regenerative bone grafting using biocompatible mineral granules or sinus floor lifts to build up new solid bone before or during implant fixture placement.
