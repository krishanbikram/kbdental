1. Nature of Our Referral Service

KB Dental is not a dental hospital, medical corporation, or direct clinical healthcare provider. We do not employ dental practitioners to provide medical care on our behalf; instead, we facilitate introductions between patients seeking dental care and independent, licensed third-party dental practices operating across Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur districts.

2. No Medical or Dental Advice

The content provided on kbdental.xyz—including procedure descriptions, symptom overviews, cost tables, diagrams, and blog articles—is presented solely for general educational and informational purposes. It does not constitute formal medical or dental advice, clinical diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always seek the advice of a qualified dental surgeon registered with the Nepal Medical Council regarding any dental condition.

3. Independent Clinical Provider Autonomy

All clinical evaluations, diagnosis, diagnostic X-ray interpretations, treatment planning, prescription of pharmaceuticals, surgical procedures, and follow-up care are performed exclusively by independent dental clinics and licensed dentists. 4. Treatment Fees and Billing

KB Dental does not charge patients any booking fees, service charges, or referral commissions. All treatment fees, consultation charges, and material expenses are billed directly by and payable directly to the independent treating dental facility. The price estimates displayed on this website represent general valley market averages and may vary based on individual clinical evaluation.
